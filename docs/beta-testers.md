# 汉字课堂 beta — tester checklist

Open **http://43.156.63.70:8169** on your phone and on a computer. Plain HTTP for now (no domain yet), so don't reuse a password you use anywhere else.

## What to try (about 20 minutes)

- [ ] **Lessons:** open Courses → a lesson → a reading. Pinyin sits above each character in tone colours. Change the pinyin/tone settings in Settings, and check the lesson updates.
- [ ] **Character page:** tap any character. Check strokes, parts ("made of" and the radical), and words that share it.
- [ ] **Practice:** write 5 characters with your finger or mouse.
- [ ] **Pinyin match:** go to Practice → Pinyin match and play one round.
- [ ] **Sentences:** go to Practice → Sentences and build a sentence from tiles.
- [ ] **Review:** run through a flashcard deck.
- [ ] **Textbooks:** go to Courses → Textbooks, paste a word list (one word per line), then practise it and review it.
- [ ] **My words:** save a few words, then on My words try **Export PDF** and **Worksheet**.
- [ ] **Worksheet:** go to Tools → Worksheet, type some characters and make a PDF.
- [ ] **Tools and Guides:** try the pinyin converter, HSK check, Chinese name and zodiac tools, and read one guide.
- [ ] **Dark mode:** switch your phone to dark mode and check every page is readable.
- [ ] **Accounts and sync:** create an account (any email, it isn't verified), practise on your phone, then sign in on the computer. Your progress should appear there too.
