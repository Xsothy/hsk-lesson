## 不: habits, facts, wishes

Use 不 for general truths, preferences and future intentions.

```zh
我不喝咖啡。 | I don't drink coffee.
明天他不来。 | He isn't coming tomorrow.
```

## 没: it did not happen

Use 没 (usually with 有) for things that did not occur, and for "have not".

```zh
我昨天没去。 | I didn't go yesterday.
他没有钱。 | He has no money.
```

## Remember

- 有 can only be negated with 没: 没有.
- Before a fourth-tone syllable, 不 is read as second tone: 不去 (bú qù).
