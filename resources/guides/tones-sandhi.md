Mandarin has four tones and a neutral one. Same syllable, different tone, different word.

```zh
妈 | mā, mother (high and level)
麻 | má, hemp (rising)
马 | mǎ, horse (dipping)
骂 | mà, scold (falling)
```

## Third tone before third tone

When two third tones meet, the first becomes second tone.

```zh
你好 | nǐ hǎo is spoken ní hǎo
```

## 不 and 一 change before a falling tone

```zh
不对 | bú duì
```
