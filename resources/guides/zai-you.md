title: 再 vs 又
summary: Both mean "again". One looks ahead, the other looks back.

Both can be translated as "again", but the time direction differs.

## 再: it has not happened yet

Use 再 for a repeat you want, plan or request in the future.

```zh
请再说一遍。 | Please say it once more.
明天再来吧。 | Come again tomorrow.
```

## 又: it already happened

Use 又 for a repeat that has already taken place (or a habit that keeps recurring).

```zh
他昨天来了，今天又来了。 | He came yesterday and came again today.
你又迟到了！ | You're late again!
```

## Rule of thumb

Future or wish: 再. Past or already-true: 又.
