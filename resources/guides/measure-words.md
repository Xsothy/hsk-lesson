---
title: Measure words 个, 本, 只
summary: Chinese counts nouns with a measure word between the number and the noun.
level: beginner
tags: measure-words, grammar
order: 5
---
Pattern: **number + measure word + noun**.

## 个 is the all-purpose one

```zh
一个人 | one person
三个苹果 | three apples
```

## 本 is for bound things like books

```zh
两本书 | two books
这本杂志 | this magazine
```

## 只 is for animals (and one of a pair)

```zh
一只猫 | one cat
两只小鸟 | two little birds
```

When unsure, 个 is understood almost everywhere, but the specific word sounds more natural.
