了 does not simply mean "past tense". It signals completion or change.

## After a verb: the action is done

```zh
我吃了饭。 | I ate (the meal).
他买了一本书。 | He bought a book.
```

## At the end of a sentence: a new situation

```zh
天黑了。 | It has gotten dark.
我不去了。 | I'm not going any more.
```

## Not for everything

Habits and general facts usually do not take 了.

```zh
我每天喝茶。 | I drink tea every day.
```
