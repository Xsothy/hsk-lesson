---
title: 的 vs 得 vs 地
summary: Three particles, all pronounced de, each with a different job.
level: elementary
tags: particles, grammar
order: 1
---
All three sound the same (*de*), but they sit in different spots. Ask what the word is attached to.

## 的 links a describer to a noun

Think "of" or "'s": it comes right before the thing being described.

```zh
我的书 | my book
很大的房子 | a very big house
他买的苹果 | the apples he bought
```

## 得 follows a verb to say how well

It comes after the verb and introduces how the action goes.

```zh
她说得很快。 | She speaks very fast.
你写得真好！ | You write really well!
```

## 地 turns a describer into "-ly" before a verb

It sits between the describing word and the verb.

```zh
他慢慢地走。 | He walks slowly.
她高兴地笑了。 | She laughed happily.
```

## Quick test

- Next to a noun after it? Use 的.
- Right after a verb, then a description? Use 得.
- Right before a verb? Use 地.
